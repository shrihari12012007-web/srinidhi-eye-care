# Srinidhi Eye Care & Opticals — Healthcare Web Portal

Srinidhi Eye Care & Opticals is a modern healthcare web application and digital atelier for the premier eye clinic in Vidya Nagar, Gangavathi, providing seamless patient appointment scheduling, interactive 3D virtual spectacle exploration, precision optical lens consulting (including bluecut digital protection and German Zeiss/Essilor optics), and an administrative portal for clinic management and patient records.
